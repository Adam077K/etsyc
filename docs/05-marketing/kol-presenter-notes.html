⚠ Before you go on — 4 quick things

  • Slide 1 is intentionally blank. No scripted line — open however feels right in the room (or hold for the team's cold open). Your first spoken words land on slide 2.
  • Confirm Sharon's written OK to use her name, "Two Dots," and her footage publicly before you present.
  • This cut has no numbers on screen. If a judge asks the business model or your cut: "It's an add-on. We take a minority slice of the new sales the story drives — Etsy keeps about 80%, plus every existing fee."
  • Slide 5's Etsy homepage is a concept (images grayed to make the point), never a spoofed live feature. Frame it as respect for what Etsy built.

Golden rules

  • Keep moving. The one-liners are punches, not paragraphs — say the line, pause, advance.
  • Let the film breathe on the journey (8–10). The maker on screen IS the wow — stop talking and let them watch for a beat.
  • Warm throughout. This cut is all story — no need to switch to "numbers mode." Sincerity is the whole pitch.
  • Never criticize Etsy — we extend them. Slide 5 is a tribute ("Etsy built the one place…"), not a knock.
  • Story and product, together. Never "meet the maker instead of the product." KOL adds the maker to the shopping.
