KOL · Final pitch · run-of-show

What to say. How to say it.

7 minutes on stage 33 slides · ~13s each — keep moving Team of 4 Fri Jul 24 2026 · Etsy Buyer-Exp panel

⚠ Before you go on — 3 quick things

  • Slide 6 typo: "So the humans disappears" → fix to "disappear" if you can before you present.
  • Slide 23 says "20 Interviews": say the number you actually ran — never inflate. A judge may ask "who did you talk to?"
  • Slide 27 graph shows old, self-inconsistent numbers ($18M→$90M, "10% take", line climbs to ~$110M). Don't read figures off it — narrate the shape. If asked your cut: "we keep a minority slice of the new revenue — Etsy keeps about 80%."

Golden rules

  • Keep moving. Most slides are 5–8 second beats. The one-liners are punches, not paragraphs — say the line, pause, advance.
  • Let the film breathe on the demo (17–20). The maker on screen IS the wow — stop talking and let them watch for a beat.
  • Warm on the human slides, crisp on the numbers. Change your energy at slide 23.
  • Never criticize Etsy — we extend them. The CMO (7) and CEO (24) quotes are Etsy agreeing with us. Deliver them with respect.
  • End on the live demo. Slide 33 is a real, working product they can scan and try. That's your mic drop.
